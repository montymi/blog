# Styx: one way into the montymi apps

Chess, NCAA Manager and LazyGrocer used to each have their own sign-in. Now there's one, called Styx. You sign in once with your Google account, and every app you've been let into knows you.

## How to join

1. **Ask.** Go to [montymi.com/join](https://montymi.com/join), enter the Google address you'll sign in with, and say which app you're after if you know.
2. **Wait.** You land on a page that's yours alone: bookmark it. I decide every request myself, within seven days.
3. **Sign in.** Once your page says it's decided, sign in with Styx using that same Google account. If you're in, you're in every app I gave you.

No email is sent along the way. Your page, and signing in, are how you find out.

## What you get

- **The apps.** Chess, NCAA Manager and LazyGrocer.
- **Your own apps, if you build them.** Members can publish an app of their own here. Their friends sign in to it with Styx, and they decide who gets in.
- **Privacy by design.** An app learns who you are to it, not your email. The one exception: when you ask to use a member's app, you're told your name and email go to them.
- **Your own network, if you'd rather.** Anyone can run their own version and, if we both agree, join mine as an equal. Neither of us is in charge of the other.

## Why it's called Styx

In the Greek myths you cross the river Styx only once you're accounted for. That's the job here: Styx says who you are, and each app decides what you can do. The other parts are named the same way, and I'll write about how they fit together another time.
